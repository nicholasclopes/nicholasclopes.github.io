Supercritical Fluids

My work with supercritical fluids (SCFs) focused on understanding and improving how we model the heat transfer mechanisms associated with such flows. We used CFD to look at a range of factors that can affect SCF cooling, including thermal boundary conditions, inlet conditions, flow development, channel geometry, buoyancy, and surface roughness. We compared CFD results with existing heat-transfer correlations and explored artificial neural networks as a way to predict SCF heat transfer behavior. These projects have helped us better understand both the underlying physics of SCF heat transfer and some of the challenges involved in modeling these flow accurately while balancing engineering practicality.
